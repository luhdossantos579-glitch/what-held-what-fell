Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09. Applies to this site and to the What Held, What Fell accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Vision Media publishes What Held, What Fell. This page lists what is collected, why, and how to request deletion.

What this site collects

Nothing about you directly. The site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies of its own and no advertising or analytics scripts. Our host (GitHub Pages) keeps ordinary server logs, including IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads, and embedded videos load from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com).

What we collect through our social accounts and the platform tools we use

We post videos, captions and replies on our own channels. Through the platforms' official tools we receive public comments and replies on our posts (name shown, comment text, time) and aggregate statistics such as views, watch time and audience age bands and countries. We do not receive your email, phone number or private messages. We use the permissions each platform grants us only to publish to our own accounts and to read the performance of our own posts. We do not read other people's accounts, and we do not collect data on users of the platforms.

What we do with it

We answer questions, remove spam, and decide which videos to make next. We do not sell, rent or share personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We keep comments only as long as the post exists, and statistics only in aggregate.

Third parties

YouTube (Google), Facebook and Instagram (Meta) and TikTok run their own services under their own policies. Affiliate links lead to sellers who may set cookies once you click; see the affiliate disclosure.

Your choices and deletion

To see, correct or delete anything we hold about you, write to contact@visionmediachannels.com with the account name and the comment or post concerned. We reply within 30 days and delete what we can identify. If you connected an account to one of our apps, you can also remove the app in your Facebook, Instagram or TikTok settings and that revokes our access immediately.

Children

Our content is general-audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. The current version is always at this address.
