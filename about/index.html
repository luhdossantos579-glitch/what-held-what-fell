About What Held, What Fell

What Held, What Fell explains engineering disasters and megaprojects from the official reports: the Hyatt walkway, Tacoma Narrows, Surfside, Gotthard, Burj.

Who makes this

What Held, What Fell is a YouTube channel published by Vision Media. Research and scripts are written in-house. The narration comes from a text-to-speech engine, which each video description states.

How we work

Each cause we state comes from a named report (NTSB, NIST, ASCE, a royal commission) or another source we cite by name. The citations are in the video description and at the bottom of every article. Diagrams are ours; photographs are public domain, openly licensed or ours, and the credits page lists them per video.
